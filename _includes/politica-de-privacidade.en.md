# Privacy Policy

**This is a translation provided for convenience. The Portuguese version is the official text and prevails in case of any discrepancy.**

**Tarot Pills** — last update: September 28, 2026

## In one sentence

The content of your readings — the question, the cards, the text you receive — remains stored **only on your device**. Two things leave it: **your question and the cards go to Google's artificial intelligence service** so the interpretation can be written, and **your credit wallet stays on our server**, because a balance worth money cannot live only on the device of the person spending it. The rest of this policy details exactly what, when, and why.

**What changed in this version.** Until August 2026 this policy said that nothing left the device except for the AI. With the introduction of credits, this is no longer true: there is now a server of ours, an anonymous identifier of yours, and a record of your purchases and credit spending. Section 4.2 is new and describes this. No information about the **content** of your readings went there.

**On September 22, 2026** the text was checked line by line against the live app, and corrected in three points: how to identify your wallet when you exercise your rights, at what point the link with the Google account is offered, and the minimum age, which is the same as the Terms of Use.

**On September 28, 2026** the contact address became contato@tgsd.com.br. The previous address ceases to be the official channel.

## 2. There is no registration, but there is an identifier

There is no login, password, email, or profile in Tarot Pills. You do not need to provide any data to use the application.

What exists, since credits became valid, is an **anonymous identifier automatically generated** the first time the application needs your wallet. It is a random code created by Firebase Authentication. **It is not your email, it is not your name, and it does not come from any of your data** — we have no way of looking at this code and knowing who you are.

What it does is just one thing: it tells whose credit balance it is.

**You can, optionally, link this identifier to your Google account.** This serves to not lose purchased credits when changing devices. The option has been in Settings since the beginning, for those who look for it. What only appears **after the first purchase** is the **warning** that there is something to lose — before that there isn't, and we do not use this warning to push the link to those who do not have paid credits at risk. **If you link it, we stop treating you as anonymous**: we get to know the email address associated with that account. Linking is your choice, and refusing does not take away any functionality other than portability itself.

## 3. What data the application processes

### 3.1 Data you provide

- **Your name or nickname.** It is optional. It only serves to make the reading written in a personal way. If you leave it blank, the application explicitly asks the AI service to treat you as someone who prefers to keep their name private.
- **The question you write** before a draw. It is optional in some draws and necessary in others, such as Yes or No.

### 3.2 Data generated by your use

- The cards drawn in each spread, with the position of each one and if they came out reversed.
- The type of spread performed: Card of the Day, Yes or No, Three Cards, Love, Career and Money, or Celtic Cross.
- The text of the interpretation received and, when applicable, the messages exchanged in the chat about that reading.
- The date and time of each reading and your streak of consecutive days.
- Your preferences: notifications, shuffling ritual, vibration, and reversed cards.
- **Your credit balance and the record of each purchase and each spend.** Unlike all the items above, this stays on the server. See section 4.2.

### 3.3 Technical data collected automatically

- **Firebase Analytics**, by Google: app usage events, a randomly generated installation identifier, device model, operating system version, language, and approximate country derived from the IP address. It serves to understand which parts of the application are used.
- **Firebase Crashlytics**, by Google: in case of a crash, the technical error report, including the point in the code where it occurred, the device model, and the system version. It serves to fix defects.
- **Firebase App Check with Play Integrity**, by Google: an attestation that the application installation is legitimate, used to prevent third parties from consuming the AI service on our behalf.
- **Firebase Authentication**, by Google: creates and stores the anonymous identifier described in section 2.
- **Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions**, by Google, on servers in the São Paulo region: they store and handle your credit wallet. See section 4.2.

None of these services receives your question, your name, or the text of your readings.

## 4. What leaves your device

This is the most important section of this policy. Read it carefully. There are two different destinations, with different contents.

### 4.1 To Google's artificial intelligence service

To generate the interpretation of a spread, the application sends to the **Firebase AI Logic service, by Google, which uses the Gemini models**:

- Your name, if you have filled it in.
- **The question you wrote**, in full, exactly as typed.
- The cards drawn, with the position of each one and if they came out reversed.
- The type of spread and the language in which the response should be written.
- When you continue the conversation in the chat, **the messages you write there**, along with the context of the reading in question.

**Write your question with this in mind.** The question is, almost always, the most intimate information that passes through the application, and it leaves your device. Avoid including data that you do not want to transmit to a third-party service — especially health data, financial data, passwords, or information that identifies other people.

### 4.2 To our server — the credit wallet

The wallet server runs on **Google's Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions, in the São Paulo region**. It receives, and stores:

- **The anonymous identifier** described in section 2, or the Google account email, if you have linked it.
- **Your credit balance**, and each movement of it: how much went in, how much went out, and when.
- **A number that identifies the reading** inside your device, so that the credit reserved for it can be settled later.
- ⚠️ **The type of the spread** — Card of the Day, Yes or No, Three Cards, Love, Career and Money, or Celtic Cross. It leaves because the price in credits depends on it, and **who decides the price must be the server**: if the application informed how much it costs, one would simply change it to pay less. **This means that we know the category of your question, even if we do not know the question.** If you drew a Love reading, that is on our server.
- **The purchase receipt issued by Google Play**, when you buy credits, so that we can verify with Google that the purchase existed before crediting.

The server **does not** receive: your question, your name, the drawn cards, the interpretation text, the chat messages, your reading history, your preferences, or your day streak.

The application **cannot write** directly to your wallet: the server rules refuse any writing coming from the device. Every credit movement goes through a function on the server, and that is why a modified device cannot create credits for itself.

### 4.3 International transfer

Processing by Google is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. The AI service runs on servers outside Brazil, which characterizes international data transfer, permitted by Article 33 of the LGPD. **The credit wallet was deliberately placed on servers in Brazil** (region `southamerica-east1`, São Paulo).

## 5. What never leaves your device

- The **complete history of your readings** — question, cards, interpretation, and chat —, which stays in a local database on the device itself.
- Your preferences and settings.
- Your streak of consecutive days.

This information is not sent to any server, is not copied to the cloud, and cannot be recovered by us. **If you uninstall the application or change devices, this data is lost and there is no way to restore it** — neither by us, nor by you.

⚠️ **This applies to readings, and not to credits.** Credits survive uninstallation if you have linked the Google account, and are lost along with the anonymous identifier if you have not. This is the reason the link is offered.

## 6. Notifications

If you keep notifications turned on, the application schedules a daily Card of the Day reminder. The scheduling happens entirely on the device, and the notification does not carry reading content. You can turn them off at any time in Settings, or in the system settings.

## 7. Credits, subscription, and payments

The purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play**, according to Google's terms. The application does not see, does not receive, and does not store your card number, your bank details, or your billing address.

What happens after the purchase: Google Play issues a **receipt**, the application sends this receipt to our server, and the server **verifies with Google** that that purchase exists, is yours, and has not been used before — only then do the credits enter your wallet. That is the reason the receipt leaves the device.

Regarding the subscription, the application checks with Google Play if there is an active subscription associated with the device's Google account.

The commercial rules for credits — validity, spending cap, what consumes them, and what does not consume them — are in the **Terms of Use**, section 7, and not here.

## 8. Who the data is shared with

We do not sell, rent, or transfer personal data. The only third parties involved are Google services, listed above, each with a specific purpose: generating the interpretation, storing the wallet, verifying purchases, measuring usage, crash reporting, integrity attestation, and payment processing.

There are no advertising networks in the application, and no advertising identifier is collected.

## 9. Legal basis for processing

- **Execution of a contract**, article 7, item V, of the LGPD: sending the question and the cards to the AI service, without which the interpretation cannot exist; and storing the credit wallet, without which there is no way to deliver what was purchased.
- **Compliance with a legal obligation**, article 7, item II: keeping the purchase record, required by consumer and tax legislation.
- **Consent**, article 7, item I: notifications, optional filling of your name, and linking with the Google account.
- **Legitimate interest**, article 7, item IX: usage metrics and crash reports, processed in an aggregated manner and without identifying you.

## 10. For how long the data is kept

The **local history** remains on the device until you delete it, in **Settings → Data → Delete All History**, or uninstall the application.

The **wallet record** — balance and movements — remains as long as your wallet exists, and the **purchases** record is kept for five years from each purchase, a period set by article 27 of the Código de Defesa do Consumidor (Brazilian Consumer Defense Code). This is the only data we do not delete upon request, and the reason is that it is the proof that you purchased.

Data sent to Google's AI service follows the retention periods of Google's policies.

## 11. Your rights

The LGPD guarantees you, in article 18, the right to obtain confirmation of the existence of processing, access to the data, correction of incomplete or outdated data, anonymization or elimination of unnecessary data, portability, information about sharing, and revocation of consent.

In practice, in Tarot Pills, this is divided into two:

- **The history of your readings is in your hands**, inside the application, without needing to ask anyone. Access and deletion are immediate.
- **The credit wallet is with us.** To obtain a copy of your credit movements, correct them, or request the deletion of the wallet, write to contato@tgsd.com.br. The response time is up to 15 days. ⚠️ **We need to be able to tell which wallet is yours**, and how to do this depends on your situation: if you **linked the Google account**, write from the linked address itself, which the application shows in Settings; if you **bought credits without linking**, provide the **Google Play order ID**, which is in the purchase confirmation email. ⛔ **If you have never linked the account and never made a purchase, your wallet is identified only by an anonymous code that lives on the device, and we have no way to link it to you remotely** — in this case it contains only free credits, and deleting the application data makes this wallet unreachable for everyone, including us. ⚠️ **Deleting the wallet erases unused credits, including purchased ones, and there is no going back.** We will tell you this again before doing it.

## 12. Children and adolescents

Tarot Pills is not directed at children or adolescents. The Terms of Use establish **18 years** as the minimum age of use, and the application does not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. The content is for entertainment and assumes a reader with the maturity to interpret it as such.

## 13. Security

Communications with Google services take place over an encrypted connection. Local data is kept in the application's private area, protected by the Android system and inaccessible to other applications on devices that have not been modified.

The credit wallet has two protections worth naming: **the device does not write to it** — the server rules refuse any writing coming from the application, and every movement goes through a function on the server —, and **each request is attested by Play Integrity**, which verifies that it comes from a legitimate installation of the application.

No security measure is absolute, and we cannot guarantee the security of a compromised device.
