### 7.2 What does not consume credit

- **The Card of the Day**, which is free every day.
- **A reading that falls into the fallback text.** When the AI service does not respond, the application presents a previously written text signaled on the screen — and **does not charge for it**.
- **Repeating the same question within 30 days.** The application returns the previous reading, with the same cards and the same interpretation, and **does not consume credit**. This is not a disguised limit: it is what the tarot tradition asserts, and the application takes it seriously.
- **A reading refused by the model** for any reason.

### 7.3 ⚠️ The reservation, and the only case in which you lose a credit without seeing a reading

Before calling the artificial intelligence service, the application **reserves** the reading's credit. When the reading arrives, the reservation becomes a spend; when it falls into the fallback text or is refused, it is returned.

**If the application is closed, dies, or loses the connection between the reservation and the response, the reservation is not returned: it becomes a spend after ten minutes.**

We say this clearly because it is the only case in which you can lose a credit without having read anything. The reason is that the reservation is only born **when the call to the AI service is actually going to happen** — at that point the cost has already been incurred on our side, and the text may have been generated without reaching your screen. Without this rule, closing the application at the right time would give unlimited readings for free, and those who pay would be the ones buying it.

If the credit is lost due to an application failure, and not because the reading was interrupted on your side, write to contato@tgsd.com.br. Your consumer rights continue to apply fully, and section 11 says that our responsibility for purchased credit is not waived by anything in these Terms.
